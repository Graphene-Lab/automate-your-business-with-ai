# Automate Your Business with AI

**A No-Jargon Guide to the Future of Work**

A practical, plain-English book that helps small-business owners, managers, and professionals understand and use Artificial Intelligence (AI) — without a technical background.

This book is written for people who run businesses, not for engineers. Every idea is explained in simple words, with real examples, step-by-step guidance, exercises, and checklists you can use the same day.

## Who this book is for

- Owners and managers of small and medium businesses (SMBs)
- Professionals and consultants who want to bring AI into daily work
- Anyone curious about AI who feels lost by technical jargon

You do **not** need to know how to code. You do **not** need a data science degree. If you can read and follow a recipe, you can follow this book.

## What you will learn

- What AI really is, where it came from, and what it can and cannot do
- How to use AI ethically and stay on the right side of the law (GDPR and the EU AI Act)
- How to keep your data safe: security, privacy, self-hosting, and digital sovereignty
- How to choose tools and vendors without being fooled
- How to run a first pilot, connect AI to the systems you already use, and measure results
- How AI helps each part of a company: finance, sales, marketing, customer care, HR, operations, IT
- Real cases from real companies, with real numbers
- A clear 90-day action plan to go from zero to your first working automation

## How the book is organized

The book has **nine parts**, **39 chapters**, and **17 practical appendices**. Each chapter is a separate Markdown file in [`books/EN/`](books/EN/).

| Part | Theme | Chapters |
|------|-------|----------|
| I | Foundations: understand where we come from | 1–3 |
| II | Ethics and responsibility | 4–5 |
| III | Security, privacy, and data sovereignty | 6–11 |
| IV | Strategy and preparation | 12–16 |
| V | Choosing and implementing | 17–20 |
| VI | Manage, measure, improve | 21–24 |
| VII | Applications by business function | 25–30 |
| VIII | Practical cases by sector | 31–36 |
| IX | Future, skills, and action plan | 37–39 |

Every regular chapter follows the same fixed structure, so you always know where to find things:

1. **In Simple Words** — the core idea, explained plainly
2. **A Bit of History** — how we got here
3. **Curiosity** — a surprising fact or story
4. **A Real Business Example** — a concrete case
5. **How to Do It** — practical steps
6. **Ethics and Responsibility** — what to keep in mind
7. **Mistakes to Avoid**
8. **Practical Exercise**
9. **Checklist**
10. **Key Takeaways** — 3–5 points to remember

Start with the [full table of contents](books/EN/README.md).

## Reading paths

- **New to AI?** Read Parts I–III in order, then skip to what you need.
- **In a hurry?** Read Chapter 12 (where to start), Chapter 18 (your first pilot), and Chapter 38 (90-day plan).
- **Worried about rules and safety?** Read Parts II and III.
- **Looking for your own industry?** Go to Part VII (by function) and Part VIII (by sector).
