# Your Assistant by Voice and by Phone

You do not always sit at a desk. AgentBridge listens when you speak, reads answers out loud, and answers a phone call like a person. This means you can use your assistant while your hands are busy or when you are away from the office.

### Speak instead of type

![A spoken note captured by the agent](../../assets/examples/dictate-memo.png)
*A spoken note captured by the agent*

**What you ask:** `(speak) Take a note: call the supplier about the delayed shipment tomorrow morning.`

The agent listens and writes the note for you. Your hands stay free; the thought is saved.

*Tip: Dictation works for messages, notes and even whole emails — just talk.*

---

### Let the agent read it to you

![The agent speaking its answer aloud](../../assets/examples/hear-the-answer.png)
*The agent speaking its answer aloud*

**What you ask:** `Read me the summary of today's orders out loud.`

The agent reads the answer in a clear voice. Useful when you are moving, cooking, or your eyes are tired.

*Tip: You can ask for any answer to be spoken, not just summaries.*

---

### Call your assistant on the phone

![A phone call to your AgentBridge assistant](../../assets/examples/call-your-agent.png)
*A phone call to your AgentBridge assistant*

**What you ask:** `(phone) What did I agree to deliver to the cafe this week?`

You call in, say what you need, and the agent answers from your notes and documents — by voice, wherever you are.

*Tip: Phone access is protected by a PIN, so only you can reach your assistant.*

---

### Hands-free on the road

![A hands-free call to check your day](../../assets/examples/hands-free-while-driving.png)
*A hands-free call to check your day*

**What you ask:** `(phone) Read me my appointment list for today.`

The agent reads your appointments over the phone so you stay focused on the road and still have your day in hand.

*Tip: Keep calls short and focused while driving; save the long work for later.*


## Key Takeaways

- You can set a job once and let AgentBridge run it, speak to it, or reach it by phone and Telegram.
- The result is the same finished work you see in the pictures, delivered where you are.
- You stay in control: you choose the time, the place, and who may use it.
