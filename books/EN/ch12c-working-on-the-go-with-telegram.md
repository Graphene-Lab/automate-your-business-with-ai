# Working on the Go with Telegram

If you already use Telegram every day, your assistant can join you there. You ask in the same chat you use with your team, and the agent answers, sends files, and keeps everyone informed. No new app to learn.

### Your assistant in Telegram

![A conversation with the agent inside Telegram](../../assets/examples/chat-on-the-go.png)
*A conversation with the agent inside Telegram*

**What you ask:** `(telegram) How much did we sell yesterday?`

The agent answers in your Telegram chat, just like a colleague. Ask a question, get the answer, keep moving.

*Tip: Telegram access uses an allow-list, so only the people you choose can talk to it.*

---

### Get a document on your phone

![A document delivered into the Telegram chat](../../assets/examples/send-a-file.png)
*A document delivered into the Telegram chat*

**What you ask:** `(telegram) Send me the invoice you made for Bright Cafe.`

The agent sends the file into your Telegram chat. Open it, forward it, or download it — right from your phone.

*Tip: You can ask for any document you made earlier and have it sent to you.*

---

### The assistant in your team group

![The agent answering in a team group chat](../../assets/examples/team-group.png)
*The agent answering in a team group chat*

**What you ask:** `(telegram group) What are today's delivery stops?`

The agent joins the group and answers the team's questions with the shared information, so nobody has to be the walking encyclopedia.

*Tip: You control who is allowed in the group and what the agent may share.*


## Key Takeaways

- You can set a job once and let AgentBridge run it, speak to it, or reach it by phone and Telegram.
- The result is the same finished work you see in the pictures, delivered where you are.
- You stay in control: you choose the time, the place, and who may use it.
