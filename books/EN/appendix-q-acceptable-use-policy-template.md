# Appendix Q — Acceptable Use Policy Template

A ready-to-adapt policy for staff who use AI at work. Copy it, fill the blanks, have your lead sign it, and share it with the team. It is short on purpose. A policy nobody reads protects nobody.

> **How to use:** Replace every `[BRACKET]` with your details. Delete what does not apply. Add what your business needs. Have it approved by `[OWNER / MANAGER]` and dated. Ask all staff to confirm they have read it.

---

## Acceptable Use of AI — [Company Name]

**Effective date:** [DATE]  **Owner:** [NAME / ROLE]  **Review:** every [6 / 12] months

### 1. Purpose

This policy sets how staff at [Company Name] may use AI tools. It protects our customers, our data, and our reputation. It applies to all staff, contractors, and anyone working on our behalf.

### 2. Approved tools only

- Staff may use **only** the AI tools on the **Approved Tools List** (Appendix: [LINK / LOCATION]).
- Using an AI tool not on the list needs **written approval** from [OWNER / ROLE] first.
- If you are unsure whether a tool is approved, **ask before you use it.** Do not guess.

### 3. Permitted uses

You **may** use approved AI tools to:

- Draft, summarise, or rewrite text (emails, reports, notes).
- Brainstorm ideas and options.
- Translate or reformat content.
- Look up general information that is not confidential.
- Automate routine, low-judgment steps that are already mapped and approved.

### 4. Prohibited uses

You **must not** use AI to:

- **Paste customer personal data** (names, addresses, emails, phone numbers, IDs, health, or financial details) into any public AI tool.
- Make a **final decision** that affects a person — hiring, firing, credit, pricing, or legal matters — without a human deciding.
- Produce content that is **illegal, harmful, deceptive, or discriminatory**.
- **Impersonate** a person or hide that AI created the content where honesty matters.
- Bypass any security control, access rule, or this policy.
- Enter **confidential company data** into tools not approved for it.

### 5. Data rules — what never to paste

Never put into any AI tool:

- Customer personal or sensitive data.
- Employee personal or sensitive data.
- Passwords, API keys, tokens, or login details.
- Financial records not already public.
- Legal documents under confidentiality.
- Trade secrets or unreleased business plans.

**Rule of thumb:** If you would not put it on a public noticeboard, do not paste it into a public AI tool.

### 6. Disclosure

- When AI output goes to a customer or the public and honesty matters, follow our disclosure rule: [STATE RULE — e.g., "note that AI assisted in drafting"].
- Never present AI work as fully human-made when that would mislead.
- Never use AI to create fake reviews, fake testimonials, or false claims.

### 7. Human review (the golden rule)

- **Every AI output must be checked by a human before it leaves the company** — to a customer, a payment, a filing, or the public.
- AI can be wrong with total confidence. You are responsible for what you send.
- For anything with money, legal, or safety weight, a **named person must approve** before it goes out.
- If you cannot check whether the AI is right, do not use its answer.

### 8. Accountability

- **You are responsible** for what you do with AI, just as you are for any other tool.
- "The AI did it" is not a defence. You chose to use it and to send its output.
- Keep any required records of AI use where asked.

### 9. Security

- Use your **own login** with **multi-factor authentication** on every AI tool.
- Never share logins or put company data into personal AI accounts.
- Report any suspected leak, wrong output, or odd behaviour **immediately** to [INCIDENT CONTACT].

### 10. Training

- All staff must complete the **AI-safety briefing** before using AI for work.
- New staff complete it in their first week.
- Refresher training runs every [6 / 12] months.

### 11. Exceptions

- Any exception to this policy needs **written approval** from [OWNER / ROLE].
- Exceptions are logged and reviewed at the next policy review.

### 12. Enforcement

- Breaking this policy may lead to: a warning, loss of AI-tool access, retraining, or disciplinary action, depending on severity.
- Serious or repeated breaches — especially leaking customer data — may end employment or contract.
- We aim to **teach before we punish.** If you are unsure, ask. Asking is never punished.
