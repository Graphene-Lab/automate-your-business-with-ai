# Meet AgentBridge: Your Office Assistant

Everything this book describes as automation can be done with one tool: AgentBridge. It is an AI assistant for small businesses that runs on your own computer. You talk to it in plain words, and it does the work — documents, spreadsheets, email, research, and more. This short chapter shows the first steps, so the examples later in the book make sense.

### Your first conversation

![The AgentBridge terminal window, ready for your first message](../../assets/examples/first-chat.png)
*The AgentBridge terminal window, ready for your first message*

**What you ask:** `Hello! What can you help me with in my office?`

The agent answers in plain words and lists what it can do: write documents, build spreadsheets, read and send email, research the web, make presentations and more. You keep talking to it like a person. Everything happens in this one window.

*Tip: You never have to memorise anything. Press F1 at any time for help, and the bottom bar always shows what is active.*

---

### Turn the right tools on

![The tools checklist: tick what the agent may use](../../assets/examples/choose-tools.png)
*The tools checklist: tick what the agent may use*

**What you ask:** `/tools`

A simple checklist appears. You move with the arrow keys and tick a tool with the Space bar. Your choice is remembered, so next time the tool is already there. The core tools (files, history, scheduling) are always on.

*Tip: In a hurry? Type the name of a ready-made set, like /tools spreadsheet-files, and it is switched on in one step.*

---

### Choose which AI answers you

![The Model setup window: pick your AI provider and model](../../assets/examples/switch-model.png)
*The Model setup window: pick your AI provider and model*

**What you ask:** `/model`

You see your providers and the active model. Pick another one and the whole conversation continues with it. Your documents, tools and history stay exactly where they are — only the answering AI changes.

*Tip: A local model keeps your data on your own machine. A cloud model is usually sharper. You decide per conversation.*

---

### Every command in one place

![The help screen lists every command with a one-line explanation](../../assets/examples/see-commands.png)
*The help screen lists every command with a one-line explanation*

**What you ask:** `F1`

The help screen shows the commands and what each one does: /files to upload, /model to switch AI, /email to set up mail, and more. It closes with one key. No manuals, no googling.

*Tip: Typing / opens a live list that filters as you type, so you often do not need the full help screen at all.*

---

### Give the agent a document to read

![The @ palette: choose an uploaded file to attach to your message](../../assets/examples/attach-file.png)
*The @ palette: choose an uploaded file to attach to your message*

**What you ask:** `@  (then pick the file)`

The file is attached to your message. The agent reads it and answers using its real contents — summarising it, checking it, or building something new from it. Nothing leaves your machine except what you choose to send to the AI.

*Tip: You can attach several files at once. The agent keeps them for the conversation, so you can ask follow-up questions.*


## Key Takeaways

- You can set a job once and let AgentBridge run it, speak to it, or reach it by phone and Telegram.
- The result is the same finished work you see in the pictures, delivered where you are.
- You stay in control: you choose the time, the place, and who may use it.
