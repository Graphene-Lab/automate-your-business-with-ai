# Appendix N — Downloadable Modules and Templates

This appendix is a map. It lists every template in the back of this book, with one line on what each is for and when to use it. Copy any of them, print them, or fill them in on screen. They are made to be used, not read once.

**How to use this index:** Find the job you are doing right now, then jump to the matching appendix. Most jobs have one main template and one or two helpers.

## A simple order to work in

If you are starting from zero, use the templates in this order:

1. **C — Map** the process you want to change.
2. **D — Inventory** the data it touches.
3. **H — Assess** the risks.
4. **E — Compute** the ROI.
5. **B / K / P — Evaluate** the vendor.
6. **I — Pilot** it small.
7. **J — Track** it with KPIs.
8. **G / Q — Govern** it with policy.
9. **O — Secure** it with the security checklist.
10. **F — Communicate** all of it to your team.

You do not need all of them at once. Pick the one that matches the decision in front of you today.

## Tips for using the templates

- **Fill them in with your team**, not alone. The conversation is as valuable as the page.
- **Keep one owner per template.** A template with no owner is ignored.
- **Revisit them.** Risks, vendors, and results change. A filled page from last month can be wrong today.
- **Print or copy freely.** These are yours to use. Do not treat them as read-only.

**Start here:** If you only do one thing, map one process (Appendix C) and assess its risk (Appendix H). That two-step alone puts you ahead of most firms that buy AI on a hunch.
