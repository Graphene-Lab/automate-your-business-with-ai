# Scheduled Tasks: Work That Runs by Itself

Most tools wait for you to ask. AgentBridge can also work on a time you agree on. You tell it once what to do and when, and it does it every time, without being reminded. This is one of the easiest ways to save time: the task runs, and the result is waiting for you.

### A report that writes itself each morning

![A scheduled task set up to run each morning](../../assets/examples/daily-report.png)
*A scheduled task set up to run each morning*

**What you ask:** `Every weekday at 8am, give me a summary of yesterday's sales and any urgent emails.`

The agent runs the report on its own each morning and has it waiting for you. You agreed the time once; it never forgets.

> This is a recurring task. AgentBridge starts it at the agreed time and delivers the result to your chat, your email or your phone.

*Tip: You can change or stop a scheduled task any time by telling the agent.*

---

### Your week, wrapped up automatically

![A weekly wrap-up scheduled to run every Friday](../../assets/examples/weekly-summary.png)
*A weekly wrap-up scheduled to run every Friday*

**What you ask:** `Every Friday at 5pm, summarise this week's sales and list what is due next week.`

The agent prepares the weekly wrap-up automatically and sends it before you leave. Next week starts with a clear plan.

> A weekly recurring task. AgentBridge handles the timing so you handle the decisions.

### Never miss a deadline

![A recurring reminder set for a monthly deadline](../../assets/examples/recurring-reminder.png)
*A recurring reminder set for a monthly deadline*

**What you ask:** `Remind me on the 25th of every month that the supplier invoice is due.`

The agent reminds you on the day, every month. You set it once and stop worrying about the date.

> A monthly recurring reminder, delivered however you like — chat, email or phone.

---

### Watch a page for changes

![A daily watch that alerts you to changes](../../assets/examples/monitor-website.png)
*A daily watch that alerts you to changes*

**What you ask:** `Check this competitor's pricing page every day and tell me if anything changes.`

The agent checks the page on schedule and only messages you when something actually changed. No noise, just the news.

> A daily scheduled watch. It runs alone and reports only when there is something to report.


## Key Takeaways

- You can set a job once and let AgentBridge run it, speak to it, or reach it by phone and Telegram.
- The result is the same finished work you see in the pictures, delivered where you are.
- You stay in control: you choose the time, the place, and who may use it.
